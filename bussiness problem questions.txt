Bussiness question that we need to solve using sql

1. How reliable is our payment system? What share of all transactions fail, and how much money is stuck in failed payments?
2. Which bank causes the most failures? Do some sender banks (SBI, ICICI, YBL, Axis, HDFC) fail more often than others?
3. When do people pay the most? Which hours of the day and which days of the week see the highest transaction volume?
4. When do payments fail the most? Are failures higher at certain hours, such as late night or peak time?
5. Does the amount affect failure? Do high-value payments (above ₹7,500, say) fail more often than small ones?
6. How much money actually moved? What is the total successful amount, the average ticket size and the largest single payment?
7. Which banks do money flow between? Which sender bank to receiver bank pair has the most transactions and the highest value?
8. Who are the repeat users? Which senders transact more than once, and how much have they sent in total?
9. Is business growing or flat? How do daily transactions and amounts trend across the month, and which day had the biggest spike or drop?
10. Are there any suspicious patterns? Are there duplicate transaction IDs, self-payments or unusually large amounts that need a closer look?

Bussiness question to solve using python 
1. How reliable is our payment system? What share of transactions fail, and how much money is stuck in failed payments?
2. Which sender bank causes the most failures? Failure rate for each bank (SBI, ICICI, YBL, Axis, HDFC).
3. When do people pay the most? Transaction volume by hour of the day and by day of the week.
4. When do payments fail the most? Failure rate by hour of the day.
5. Does the amount affect failure? Failure rate across amount buckets (0-2,500, 2,500-5,000, 5,000-7,500, 7,500+).
6. How much money actually moved? Total, average, smallest and largest successful payment.
7. Which banks do money flow between? Transaction count, amount and success rate for each sender-bank to receiver-bank route.
8. Who are the repeat users? Senders who appear more than once, with their transaction count and total sent.
9. Is business growing or flat? Daily transaction trend and day-over-day change.
10. Are there suspicious patterns? Duplicate IDs, self-payments and unusually large amounts.
